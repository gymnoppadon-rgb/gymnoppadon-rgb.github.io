yt-uploader

yt-uploader is a private, single-user tool. It uploads video files from the owner's own computer to the owner's own YouTube channel and adds them to the owner's playlists, using the YouTube Data API.

Privacy policy

  • The tool is used only by its owner. No other users sign in to it.
  • It accesses only the owner's YouTube account, and only to upload videos and manage the owner's playlists.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
